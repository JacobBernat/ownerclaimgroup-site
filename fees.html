Fees

No fee unless you are paid

We only get paid when you do. Our fee is a share of what the state actually pays you, agreed in writing before we start.

Nothing upfront

No sign-up cost, no filing fees, no charges for documents or research.

Agreed in writing

The agreement names the property, the amount and our fee before any work on the claim begins.

Only after you're paid

The state pays you directly. If the claim isn't paid, you owe us nothing.

Never above your state's limit

Each state sets the most a recovery company may charge. Our fee is set in your agreement and is never higher than that limit. These are the legal maximums in the states we serve:

Maximum recovery fee by state
StateLegal maximum fee
Wisconsin10%
Alaska10% on claims of $500 or more; 20% under $500
Nevada10% if held 2 to 5 years; 20% if held 5+ years
New York15%
Colorado10%
New Jersey20%
WyomingNo set cap; agreed in writing up front

Limits are set by each state's unclaimed property law and can change. Your agreement always shows your exact fee.

Ask us anything before you sign. We'll explain exactly what the fee would be on your claim.
