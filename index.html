What we do

Money people don't know they have

After a few years without contact, banks, insurers and companies must send unclaimed money to the state, where it waits for the owner. Most owners never hear about it, especially when it's under an old company name, an old address, or a family member who has passed away.

Businesses

Customer refunds, credit balances, rebates and vendor checks that were never cashed or applied.

Merged or renamed companies

Money still listed under a former name. Proving the company today is the rightful owner is where we help most.

Families and estates

Accounts, dividends and insurance left by someone who has died, where heirs need the right documents to claim.
