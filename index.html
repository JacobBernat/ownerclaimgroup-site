FAQ

Straight answers.

What people ask us most. Anything else, just call.

Can I claim it myself?

Yes. Every state has its own process, and owners can always file a claim directly with the state for free. People choose us because the research, the documents and the back-and-forth with the state take time they would rather spend elsewhere, and because there is no fee unless the claim is paid.

Are you a government agency?

No. Owner Claim Group is a private company. We are not part of, and not affiliated with, any state unclaimed property office or government agency.

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. Our fee is one agreed share of what the state actually pays you, written into your agreement before we start and never above your state's legal limit. If the state doesn't pay, you owe nothing.

How do I know you're legitimate?

Ask that of anyone who contacts you about money. We never ask for payment upfront, never ask for bank or account logins, and never ask for gift cards or wire transfers. Before you sign anything, we show you exactly what we found: the property, which state holds it and about how much. The state pays you directly, not us.

How did you find me?

States publish lists of unclaimed property and the names it's held under. We search those public records and reach out when we find a match for you, your business, or a family member.

Who pays me, you or the state?

The state pays you directly. We're paid our agreed fee only after you've received your money.

How long does it take?

It depends on the state and the claim. Reviews can take a few weeks to several months, and estate or company-history claims take longer than simple ones. You can follow every stage in your client portal.

What will you need from me?

Usually the signed agreement, the state's claim form, a photo ID for the signer, and records that link you to the name and address on file. For a business, a W-9. For a merged or renamed company or an estate, the documents that show the change or the heirs. We tell you exactly what your claim needs.

Will you ask for my Social Security number?

Never through this website, by text, or in a plain email. Some state claim forms ask for it to confirm your identity. When that happens, you write it on the state's form yourself, as part of the claim you sign.

Are you lawyers?

No. We are not a law firm and don't give legal or tax advice. If a claim needs a court process, such as opening an estate, we'll tell you so you can speak with an attorney.

Let's find what's yours.

Send us your name or business name, plus any former names. A real person will check and tell you what we find. Asking is free and commits you to nothing.

Please don't send your Social Security number or bank details. We never ask for them here.
