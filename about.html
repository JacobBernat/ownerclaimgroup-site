About us

We help people get back what's theirs

Owner Claim Group finds money that states are holding for people, families and businesses, and handles the claim from start to finish.

Why we do this

Billions of dollars sit with state treasuries because the owner moved, a company changed its name, or a family member passed away and nobody knew about the account. The money is real, but finding it and proving it's yours takes time, records and patience.

That's the part we do. We focus on the people and businesses who don't know they have money waiting, or don't know how to get to it.

How we work

  • We follow each state's rules on recovery agreements and fees
  • We tell you everything up front and in plain words
  • We never ask for payment before you're paid
  • We protect your documents and never sell your information
