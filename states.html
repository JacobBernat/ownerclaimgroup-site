States we serve

We work within each state's rules

Every state runs its own unclaimed property program, with its own forms, timelines and limits on fees. These are the states where we currently file claims.

WI

Wisconsin

Fee limit: 10%

Recovery agreements apply once the money has been with the state for 24 months.

AK

Alaska

Fee limit: 10% on claims of $500 or more; 20% under $500

Agreements run no longer than 6 months.

NV

Nevada

Fee limit: 10% if held 2 to 5 years; 20% if held 5+ years

No fee on money held less than 24 months.

NY

New York

Fee limit: 15%

The agreement is signed in front of a notary.

CO

Colorado

Fee limit: 10%

Recovery agreements apply once the money has been with the state for 24 months.

NJ

New Jersey

Fee limit: 20%

Recovery agreements apply once the money has been with the state for 24 months.

WY

Wyoming

Fee limit: No set cap; agreed in writing up front

Agreements are in writing, with a notarized limited power of attorney.

Live in another state?

Money is often held by the state where the company that owed it was based, not where you live. Tell us your name or business name and we'll check, and tell you honestly what's possible.
